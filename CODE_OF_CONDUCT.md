# Community Code of Conduct / قواعد المجتمع

This project welcomes respectful technical collaboration from contributors of all backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, design, tests, and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Be patient with contributors who are new to Odoo, Git, English, or Arabic.
- Give actionable review feedback.
- Protect customer privacy and security information.
- Respect software licenses and third-party intellectual property.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Intentionally posting credentials, customer data, exploitable secrets, or malicious code.
- Misrepresenting testing, authorship, compatibility, certification, or project status.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove contributions that violate these rules and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

---

## العربية

نرحب بالمساهمات التقنية المحترمة من الجميع.

المطلوب هو مناقشة الكود والتصميم والاختبارات باحترام، حماية بيانات الزبائن والأسرار، واحترام التراخيص وحقوق الأطراف الأخرى. لا يُسمح بالإساءة أو التهديد أو نشر المعلومات الخاصة أو الادعاء باختبارات/اعتمادات لم تحصل فعليًا.
